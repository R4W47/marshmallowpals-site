How it plays

Reach the target score before you run out of moves. The better you do, the more stars you take home.

  • Earn up to three starsBeat your best score on every level and come back for the stars you missed.
  • Animal Rush when you winYour leftover moves turn into special pieces that clear the board for bonus points.
  • Made for one handPlays in portrait on your phone, so you can match on the go.
