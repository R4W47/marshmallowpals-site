Swap, match and make friends

Swap two neighboring pals to line up three or more of the same kind. Reach the target score before you run out of moves, and finish each level with as many stars as you can.

  • A new world every 100 levelsTravel across a map of colorful places, each with its own scenery.
  • Earn up to three starsBeat your best score on every level and come back to collect the stars you missed.
  • A party when you winLeftover moves turn into special pieces that clear the board for bonus points.
  • Made for one handPlays in portrait on your phone, so you can match on the go.
