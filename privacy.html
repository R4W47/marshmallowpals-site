Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how the game Marshmallow Pals ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. We built the game to be enjoyed without sharing personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it does not require an account.

Game progress saved on your device

Your progress, such as completed levels, stars and best scores, is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress will be deleted.

Advertising and analytics

The game does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools. If this changes in a future update, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and describe which services are used and what information they handle.

Children's privacy

Marshmallow Pals is suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has somehow shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information as part of providing its store and services, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
