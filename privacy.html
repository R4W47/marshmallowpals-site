Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how the game Marshmallow Pals ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. The game does not ask you for any personal information and does not require an account. To keep the game free, it shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and this policy explains what that involves.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share personal information ourselves. The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

Game progress saved on your device

Your progress, such as completed levels, stars, best scores, lives, power-ups and settings (language, music and sound), is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is deleted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch a short video to get an extra life or a power-up. Other ads may appear between levels.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with, and diagnostic data such as crash information related to ads.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is used according to Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive information that identifies you personally.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the game's home screen.
  • Personalized ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

Children's privacy

Marshmallow Pals is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept by Google as described in its own policies. Data sent to Google AdMob is encrypted in transit.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information as part of providing its store and services, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If we add new services that handle information, we will update this policy first.
